Behavioral Interview

Imagine that you have an upcoming interview for a job or internship that interests you. The interviewer wants to hear about situations you have experienced and how you handled them. You will respond to behavioral interview questions from an AI interviewer. You do not need experience in a particular industry.

What to expect:

The AI interviewer will ask questions one at a time. It may ask a follow-up about your example or share a brief comment before moving on. Follow the instructions on each screen to move through the study.

How to answer:

  • Examples from work, school, volunteering, or other activities are all welcome.
  • Describe what happened, what you personally did, and what happened as a result.
  • Type in your own words. You can ask the interviewer to repeat a question if needed.
  • If you do not have an example or prefer not to answer, say so and continue.

    • Keep in mind: This is a simulated interview not a job application. A small, everyday example is fine. You do not need to invent an experience or include anyone's name or other identifying details.

    Begin when prompted by the study interface and you are ready.

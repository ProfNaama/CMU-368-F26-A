Imagine that you have an upcoming interview for a job or internship that interests you. The interviewer wants to hear about situations you have experienced and how you handled them. You will respond to behavioral interview questions from an AI interviewer. The following interview does not assess any technical knowledge, and no prior experience or industry knowledge is required.

What to expect:

The AI interviewer will ask 5 behavioral questions one at a time. After each answer, the interviewer will share brief feedback on your response before moving on to the next question. Please respond to the questions as you would in a real practice interview, and follow the instructions on each screen to move through the study.

How to answer:
  • Examples from work, school, volunteering, or other activities are all welcome.
  • You may reuse the same example for more than one question, at any point in the session.
  • Describe what happened, what you personally did, and what happened as a result.
  • Please answer by speaking. Click Speak, say your answer out loud, then click Stop. Your browser will ask to use your microphone, so please allow it. Voice answers work in Chrome, Edge, and Safari.
  • Your answer will then appear as text. Use the keyboard only to correct mistakes in that text, such as wrong or missing words, before you send it. Please keep your original wording and meaning.
  • You can ask the interviewer to repeat a question if needed.
  • If you do not have an example or prefer not to answer, say so and continue.
Important
  • Please do not include anyone's name or other identifying details.
  • Please do not refresh the page or use your browser's back button during the session.

When you are ready, click Speak and say "ready" to begin.

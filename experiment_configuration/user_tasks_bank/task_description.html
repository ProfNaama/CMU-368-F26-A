Behavioral Interview

Imagine that you have an upcoming interview for a job that interests you. The interview is a behavioral interview, which means the interviewer wants to hear about situations you have experienced and how you handled them. Below is a practice interview with an AI interviewer. It does not assess any technical knowledge, and no prior experience or industry knowledge is required.

What to expect:

The AI interviewer will ask 6 behavioral questions one at a time. After each answer, the interviewer will share brief feedback on your response before moving on to the next question. Please respond to the questions as you would in a real practice interview, and follow the instructions on each screen to move through the study.

How to answer:
  • Examples from work, volunteering, or other everyday activities are all welcome.
  • Describe what happened, what you personally did, and what happened as a result.
  • Type in your own words. You can ask the interviewer to repeat a question if needed.
  • If you do not have an example or prefer not to answer, say so and continue.
Keep in mind: This is a simulated interview, not a job application. A small, everyday example is fine, and you do not need to invent an experience. Please remember not to include personal identifiers or personal information.

Begin when prompted by the study interface and you are ready.
